Small pilot checklist

Suggested two-week rollout

Before the first learner sees it

□
Confirm facts
Verify joining route, programme descriptions, scholarship status, contact channel and response times.
□
Assign owners
Name an FAQ owner and a human support owner; agree on hand-off response expectations.
□
Choose a channel
Decide whether the first release belongs on the website, a form or a messaging platform.
□
Review privacy
Set collection, consent, retention and access rules before capturing real learner details.
